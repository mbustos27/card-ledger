Pile values count unchecked cards at LP. Unchecked cards are sorted into piles by their NM value.

    Piles. Toploader: value $20 and up. Small binder (penny sleeve): $2 to under $20. Big binder (Pokédex order): under $2 and holo, reverse holo, rare, promo or better. Box: other commons and uncommons, every extra copy of a card, and cards with no price. Set aside: kept but left out of the piles.

    Extra copies. One copy of each card is the keeper: the best condition among checked copies, or copy 1 until any is checked. The other copies go to the box; their value still counts.

    Unchecked cards are sorted into piles by their NM value and count in the totals at their LP value. Their range runs from NM down to HP.

    Condition values come from per-condition prices where a source has them, then research ranges, otherwise NM × a condition multiplier. Each value names its basis.
